How to play

  1. The facilitator hosts the game on the shared screen. Everyone joins on their phone with the room code.
  2. The card on the table shows which bin is in play. On your turn, tap a card from your hand that goes in the same bin. Your cards hide their bin!
  3. Right bin: +10 points. Galat bin: the card shows its real bin, −5 points, you draw 1 and your turn ends.
  4. No match? Tap Draw. If time runs out, you draw automatically.
  5. 🍃 Vanesh Wild can be played any time. You choose the next bin.
  6. On your last card, tap SWACHH! within 8 seconds (+5) or draw 2.
  7. Empty your hand first (+50). The game ends when three players have finished or the clock runs out. Then the highest scores win.
